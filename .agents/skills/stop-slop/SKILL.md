# Stop Slop

Use this as a final prose pass, not as a substitute for factual review.

- Cut greetings, throat-clearing, repetition, vague intensifiers, and empty
  transitions.
- State concrete actors, actions, evidence, and limits.
- Prefer direct sentences over canned contrasts or dramatic fragments.
- Keep technical terms, identifiers, commands, warnings, and necessary caveats
  exact; do not simplify away safety or precision.
- Vary sentence structure when it improves readability. Do not apply mechanical
  bans on adverbs, passive voice, or question-led sentences.

Complete when every retained sentence adds meaning, evidence, instruction, or a
necessary transition.
